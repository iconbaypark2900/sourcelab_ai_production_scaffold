# AI Risk Taxonomy Overview

## Categories

AI risk is commonly organized into capability risks, misuse risks, alignment risks, and systemic risks. Capability risks arise when models gain abilities that enable harm at scale. Misuse risks involve deliberate exploitation by actors. Alignment risks occur when systems optimize the wrong objective or behave unpredictably. Systemic risks include concentration of power, labor disruption, and cascading failures across interconnected systems.

## Assessment Approach

Risk assessment should map model capabilities to deployment context, identify failure modes, and prioritize controls based on severity and likelihood. A taxonomy helps teams communicate consistently about risk without treating all issues as equally urgent.

## Mitigation Priorities

Start with inventory of model use cases, classify data sensitivity, and apply tiered controls. Document assumptions, monitor drift, and plan incident response before scaling access.

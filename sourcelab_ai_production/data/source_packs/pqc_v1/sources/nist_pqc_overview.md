# NIST Post-Quantum Cryptography Standardization Overview

## Background

NIST initiated the Post-Quantum Cryptography Standardization process in 2016 to evaluate and standardize quantum-resistant public key algorithms. The goal is to develop cryptographic standards that can withstand attacks from both classical and quantum computers.

## Timeline

- 2016: NIST PQC Standardization process initiated
- 2022: First standards published (FIPS 203, 204, 205)
- 2024-2025: Additional algorithms under evaluation
- 2030: Target date for transition recommendations

## Key Documents

- FIPS 203: Module-Lattice-Based Key-Encapsulation Mechanism Standard (ML-KEM)
- FIPS 204: Module-Lattice-Based Digital Signature Standard (ML-DSA)
- FIPS 205: Stateless Hash-Based Digital Signature Standard (SLH-DSA)

## Recommendations

Organizations should:
1. Begin cryptographic inventory immediately
2. Prioritize high-value, long-lived data
3. Plan for hybrid implementations during transition
4. Test PQC algorithms in non-production environments first
